Realistic Calculation

If you write 20 emails/day:

  • Without AI: 20 × 15 min = 300 min = 5 hours/day
  • With BoosterMail: 20 × 2 min = 40 min = 0.67 hours/day
  • Savings: 4.3 hours/day (!)

But you probably have 8-10 "serious" emails and 10-12 "quick" ones. Let's recalculate:

  • Serious (15 min → 3 min) × 8 = 96 min saved
  • Quick (8 min → 1 min) × 12 = 84 min saved
  • Total: 180 min = 3 hours/day

But Be Careful...

Not all emails are worth equal time.

Simple email ("OK"): 2 min normally → 30 sec with AI = 1.5 min savings.
Complex email (contract renegotiation): 45 min normally → 15 min with AI = 30 min savings.

Your real savings depend on your email mix.

💡 Formula: Savings = (number of emails × average time) - (number of emails × time with AI) = significant difference, especially if you write a lot and have "blocking" emails.
