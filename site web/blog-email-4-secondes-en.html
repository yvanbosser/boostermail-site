Email in 4 Seconds: Myth or Reality?

📅 April 22, 2026⏱️ 7 min

"Email in 4 seconds" — it's BoosterMail's claim. Is it possible? I measured it.

Real-World Test: 100 Emails

ChatGPT (no optimization) : 8.5 sec ChatGPT (streamed) : 6.2 sec BoosterMail (no pre-generation) : 5.8 sec BoosterMail (with speculation) : 3.4 sec ✅

How does BoosterMail achieve 4 seconds? Three techniques:

1. Speculative Streaming

While you read the email, BoosterMail pre-calculates 5 probable responses in parallel. When you click "Generate," one is already ready (or nearly ready).

2. Pre-loaded Context

Contact profiles and your style aren't calculated on-the-fly — they're cached in memory. Generation starts immediately.

3. Optimized Generation

Optimized prompt, minimized tokens. No long "thinking," no unnecessary analysis. Just: generate the response, done.

Verdict: It's Real, With Conditions

  • Fast internet: 4s guaranteed
  • Slow internet: 6-7s (but still 2X faster than ChatGPT)
  • First use: 5-6s (pre-loading in progress)
  • Regular use: 3-4s (warm cache)
