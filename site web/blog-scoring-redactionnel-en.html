What is Writing Score?

Writing score is an automated system that evaluates the quality of your emails. Unlike basic spell-checkers, it analyzes:

  • Logical structure (message clarity)
  • Tone (professionalism, empathy)
  • Relevance (context and recipient adaptation)
  • Impact (ability to trigger action)
  • Personalization (recognition of your personal style)

Unlike generic systems that only check spelling, our Claude AI system analyzes the why behind email success or failure at a psychological and relational level.

The 5 Key Scoring Criteria

1. Clarity (0-20pts)

Can the recipient understand your request in 10 seconds?

2. Relevance (0-20pts)

Is your reply adapted to your contact's profile?

3. Tone (0-20pts)

Does your tone match your usual style?

4. Conciseness (0-20pts)

Have you said what matters without verbosity?

5. Call-to-Action (0-20pts)

Is the next step clear for the recipient?

Each criterion is scored out of 20 points, for a total of 100. All criteria are weighted equally.

From Beginners to Experts: 10 Levels (N1-N10)

Beyond the raw 0-100 number, your score places your email in a competency category:

  • N1-N2 (0-20): Confused, unprofessional email. Needs complete revision.
  • N3-N4 (21-40): Acceptable but awkward. Several improvement areas.
  • N5-N6 (41-60): Correct and clear. Works, but not persuasive.
  • N7-N8 (61-80): Well-structured and professional. Good reply probability.
  • N9-N10 (81-100): Excellent email. Persuasive, adapted, memorable. Very high odds of positive reaction.

How to Improve Your Score

1. Simplify Your Opening

❌ Bad: "Following our multiple conversations over the past three weeks regarding the strategic project…"

✅ Good: "Hi John, on the Q2 project…"

2. One Idea = One Sentence

Each sentence should carry one idea. Bullet points help clarify (typically +15 score points).

3. Visible Personalization

Mention a detail from your last exchange. That's the difference between N7 (good) and N10 (excellent).

4. Specific Call-to-Action

❌ Bad: "Let me know what you think"

✅ Good: "Can you confirm availability Monday or Tuesday to validate?"

5. Proofread with Fresh Eyes

Wait 5 minutes before reviewing. You'll catch mistakes you missed.
