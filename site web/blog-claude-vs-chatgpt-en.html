Detailed Results

1. Writing Quality (most important)

Claude wins. Claude emails sound more natural, more professional. Less "AI buzzwords" like "I'd be delighted to..."

Example:

❌ ChatGPT: "I would be delighted to assist you with your inquiry regarding the Q2 business proposal..."
✅ Claude: "On your Q2 proposal, I have a few adjustments. Available Thursday to discuss?"

2. Style Personalization

Claude shines: it truly adapts tone to your history. ChatGPT does "best effort" but less precise.

3. Speed

ChatGPT faster (streaming better optimized). Claude slightly slower but quality > speed for emails.

4. Long Context

Claude can read 100K tokens (~75,000 words). ChatGPT 128K. For emails: Claude sufficient and better for relational analysis.

5. Cost

Claude: $3 per million tokens (input).
ChatGPT: $10 per million tokens.
Gemini: $0.075 per million tokens (practically free).

But: If Claude generates a better response on the first try (no revisions), you save overall.

6. GDPR & Privacy

Claude: Mistral option in Europe (France hosting, GDPR compliant).
ChatGPT: USA, data potentially for OpenAI training (verify legally).
Gemini: USA also.

🔐 GDPR Verdict: If you handle sensitive data (contracts, salaries), Claude + Mistral is legally safer.

Which Engine for Your Case?

Choose Claude if:

  • You write a lot (quality > cost)
  • You work in Europe or need GDPR
  • Your writing style needs hyper-personalization
  • You have long context to analyze

Choose ChatGPT if:

  • Speed is crucial (ultra-fast reactions)
  • You prefer OpenAI ecosystem
  • You have minimal sensitive data

Choose Gemini if:

  • Cost is the decisive factor
  • You prefer Google ecosystem
  • Simple, standard, non-critical emails
