Automatic Contact Profile: Know Who You're Writing To in 3 Seconds

📅 April 22, 2026 ⏱️ 8 min read ✍️ By BoosterMail

You never respond the same way to the CEO and the intern. But how can AI know that?

Before generating your response, BoosterMail automatically builds the relational profile of your contact. In 3 seconds, the system analyzes:

  • What is their hierarchical position (CEO, manager, peer, junior)?
  • What is the exchange history (how many emails, over what duration)?
  • What is the relational context (friend, colleague, client, prospect)?
  • What tone do you typically use with this person?
  • What are the sensitive or critical topics?

Result: the generated response isn't just "professional" — it's specifically adapted to this person.

Why Contact Profile Is Critical

Imagine two absolutely identical emails except for content:

Scenario 1: Email from your boss asking for the Q1 report.

Expected response: Formal, brief, with exact deadlines.

Scenario 2: Email from your developer friend asking for advice.

Expected response: Casual, conversational, maybe with emojis.

Without contact profile, generic AI responds the same to both. With contact profile, it's perfect for each one.

How BoosterMail Automatically Builds the Profile

1. Hierarchical Analysis (Outlook Metadata)

BoosterMail reads Outlook metadata: contact's department, person's title, level in organizational chart. If Laurent is "VP Sales" and you're "Sales Rep," the AI adjusts tone of authority.

2. Exchange History (Threading)

All emails with this person are analyzed: how many exchanges? Over what period? What rhythm (urgent, relaxed, sporadic)? Do you have 5 emails or 500 emails of history together?

3. Relational Context (AI Detection)

The AI asks: "Who is this person to the user?" Based on language patterns, it classifies: friend/colleague/manager/client/prospect.

4. Tone Extraction (Your Habits)

BoosterMail looks at how you typically write to this person. It's the best predictor of what they expect.

5. Sensitivity Detection (NLP)

If the history contains: disagreements, tensions, unfulfilled promises — the AI notes "sensitive situation" and adapts tonality to be more attentive, more cautious.

💡 Concrete example: You're writing to Michael, a client for 5 years. The AI detects: 12 exchanges total, no tension, tone always relaxed. Result: generated email is natural and familiar. Otherwise, if the AI saw 50 exchanges with 3 incidents, it would generate an email that's proactive, reassuring, more formal.

The 5 Dimensions of Contact Profile

Dimension 1: Required Formality (0-10)
0 = "Hey!" | 5 = "Hello," | 10 = "Dear Sir or Madam,"
The AI adapts greeting, punctuation, vocabulary.

Dimension 2: Perceived Urgency (0-10)
0 = "No rush, reply whenever" | 10 = "It's URGENT"
Changes response length and type of call-to-action.

Dimension 3: Relative Expertise (0-10)
0 = "This person is an expert, I explain little" | 10 = "Novice, I explain more"
Affects technical level and depth of detail.

Dimension 4: Emotional Closeness (0-10)
0 = "Very close, friend, relaxed tone" | 10 = "Distant, hierarchical, formal tone"
Influences emojis, humor, familiarity.

Dimension 5: Historical Tension (0-10)
0 = "Good history, we trust each other" | 10 = "Bad history, maximum caution"
Changes tonality: reassurance vs. directiveness.

In Practice: Real Example

Incoming email: "Hi, I need to renegotiate the Q2 contract. Available for a call?"

Profile 1 (Caroline, CEO, 2 exchanges, 8 months of history, formal tone):

"Dear Caroline, I'd be delighted. I'll validate the Q2 adjustments this week. Would you be available Tuesday at 2pm or Thursday at 10am? Best regards,"

Profile 2 (David, colleague, 85 exchanges, 5 years together, relaxed tone):

"Hey David! Yeah, we can set up a call. Tuesday at 2pm or Wednesday morning? I'll prep my file by then. Cheers,"

Same situation, two completely different responses — because the profiles were different.

Continuous Profile Update

The contact profile isn't static. After each email:

  • If you strongly correct the tone ➜ profile adjustment
  • If the person gets promoted (title changes) ➜ profile updated
  • If long silence followed by urgent email ➜ urgency recalibration
  • After 3 mails ➜ relational trust recalculation

Bottom line: The more you use BoosterMail with a person, the better the AI knows them. And the better it knows them, the more perfect the generated response is.
