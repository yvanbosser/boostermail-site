AI and Privacy: Keep Control of Your Data

📅 April 22, 2026⏱️ 7 min

You send your emails to an AI. Who else sees them?

Good question. It's reason #1 why many hesitate with ChatGPT or Gemini: data goes to USA, potentially for model training.

The Reality: 3 Data Tiers

Tier 1: OpenAI / Google (USA)

⚠️ ChatGPT & Gemini: Your data goes to USA servers. They promise "no training" if you're enterprise (with contract), but...

  • Data transiting USA = not 100% GDPR compliant
  • USA government can request access
  • Legal risk if sensitive data

Tier 2: Anthropic (Claude, but still transiting)

⚠️ Claude standard: Data encrypted in transit, secure servers, BUT no Europe hosting. Better than OpenAI, not 100% GDPR.

Tier 3: Mistral (Europe)

✅ Mistral + BoosterMail: 100% France hosting. Data never leaves the EU. GDPR fully respected. Most secure option.
